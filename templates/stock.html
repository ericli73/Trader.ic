Methodology & limitations
  • Technical/momentum/risk model only - no fundamentals, valuation, sentiment, or insider/institutional data yet (no free point-in-time source for those exists, see below).
  • Backtest evidence comes from a 40-stock, 10-year validation universe, not the full S&P 500 or the stock's full history.
  • Survivorship bias: the validation universe is today's large, still-listed companies - delisted/failed/acquired companies aren't represented.
  • A single chronological train/test split is used, not full walk-forward re-optimization.
  • Signal-edge scoring only rewards signals with a measured, statistically significant edge over just holding - not every textbook-bullish pattern.
